ISO - a DJ isolator EQ
by Gussa Naaman


A three-band DJ isolator EQ - the club mixer's EQ section as a plug-in.

  LOW / MID / HIGH    big band knobs, -30 (silence) to +12 dB, KILL under each
  LOW/MID, MID/HIGH   the two crossover points, also draggable on the graph
  SLOPE 12 / 24       Xone:92-style 12 dB/oct or rotary-isolator 24 dB/oct
  CUT ISO / EQ        knob floor is silence (isolator) or -26 dB (DJM EQ curve)
  FILTER + RES        bipolar sweep: left = low-pass, right = high-pass

With every band at 0 dB it is flat to 0.000 dB and adds zero latency, so you
can leave it on a channel and forget it is there.


WHERE THIS PUTS THINGS

  VST3 plug-in    C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ISO.vst3
  Standalone      C:\Program Files\Naaman\ISO\ISO.exe

Close your DAW before continuing. Windows cannot replace a plug-in that a
running host has open, and that is the usual reason an update seems to do
nothing at all.

Afterwards, in Cubase: Studio menu, VST Plug-in Manager, Update. ISO appears
under Naaman, in the EQ category.

To remove it later, use Windows' own Apps list - ISO uninstalls like any
other program.


This build is not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen may show a blue "Windows
protected your PC" panel: click "More info", then "Run anyway". The manual,
MANUAL.md, is installed alongside the plug-in.
