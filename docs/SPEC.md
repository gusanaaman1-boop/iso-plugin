# ISO — DJ isolator EQ · specification v1.0

Seventh member of the plug-in family (after EDGE, FOUR COLOR, DUAL SPACE, TRIX,
CORECOLOR, MELO TRACE). VST3 / AU / Standalone, JUCE 9, `Make Music/Iso`.

## Signal path

```
in ─┬─ LP(f1) ── AP(f2) ─────────── × LOW  ─┐
    └─ HP(f1) ─┬─ LP(f2) ─────────── × MID  ─┼─ Σ ── FILTER (LP/HP sweep) ── OUTPUT ── out
               └─ HP(f2) ─────────── × HIGH ─┘
```

* Crossover: Linkwitz-Riley. **24 dB/oct** = LR4 (two cascaded Butterworth-2
  TPT-SVF sections; low band gets a matching 2nd-order all-pass at f2 so all
  three sum to an all-pass). **12 dB/oct** = LR2 (Q 0.5 sections, mid inverted,
  1st-order all-pass on the low band).
* Unity sum measured flat to **0.000 dB** at both slopes (test 1).
* Every band gain and the trim are smoothed over 6 ms; crossovers and the
  sweep cutoff over 20 ms. Kill = gain ramp to exactly zero: click-free
  (max sample step 0.0054 on a 30 Hz tone, test 7).
* No allocations after prepare; safe at 44.1 – 192 kHz; ~2000× realtime.

## Controls

| Control | Range | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LOW / MID / HIGH | −30 … +12 dB (knob), 0 dB at 70 % of travel | 0 dB | In **ISO** mode the bottom 6 dB fades to silence, so knob-at-floor == KILL. In **EQ** mode the floor is −26 dB (Pioneer DJM EQ curve). |
| KILL ×3 | on / off | off | Latching lamp pads. |
| LOW / MID | 60 – 1000 Hz | 250 Hz | Also draggable on the graph. Clamped ≥ 1.5× below MID / HIGH. |
| MID / HIGH | 800 – 10 000 Hz | 2.5 kHz | Also draggable on the graph. |
| SLOPE | 12 / 24 dB/oct | 24 | 12 = Xone:92 style, 24 = rotary isolator. |
| CUT | ISO / EQ | ISO | Floor law for the band knobs. |
| FILTER | −1 … +1 | 0 (off) | Left: LP 20 kHz → 60 Hz. Right: HP 20 Hz → 12 kHz. ±4 % dead zone. |
| RES | 0 – 100 % | 25 % | Q 0.71 → 6, ramped in over the first 20 % of travel so a barely-turned filter cannot ring at 20 kHz. |
| OUTPUT | ±12 dB | 0 | |
| BYPASS | | off | |

Presets change **character only** (corners, slope, cut law, resonance) — never
the performance controls (gains, kills, filter). See `PARAMETER-TABLE.md`
(generated by `IsoShot --params`).

## Display

Analytic response of the *exact* filters (bilinear-exact SVF, evaluated in the
prototype domain), bands summed **with phase**, then filter and trim. Measured
vs analytic agree to 0.000 dB across five awkward settings (test 5). Each band's
own contribution is filled in its hue; killed bands go dim; the white line is
what the ear gets.

## Colour law

Low = ember `FF5A47`, Mid = gold `FFC247`, High = ice `47D6FF`, Filter =
violet `A98CFF`, neutral = `DDE3F0`. A hue appears on its band's knob arc, its
pointer, its KILL pad and its fill in the graph, and nowhere else.

## Robustness

* Non-finite input samples are scrubbed to zero before any recursive stage
  (identity on finite input) — one NaN from upstream cannot poison the filters.
* Read-outs parse back to what they print: `KILL`, `+3 dB`, `2.50 kHz`, `40 %`,
  `LP 914 Hz`, `OFF`.
* Editor: 60–200 %, aspect locked, size saved with the state.
